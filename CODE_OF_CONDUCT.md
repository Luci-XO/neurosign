# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NeuroSign aims to be a respectful, constructive space for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. We welcome participation that helps make
the project safer, clearer, and more useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks or harassment.
- Accept feedback graciously and focus on the project.
- Protect privacy: do not post personal health information, recordings, or
  identifying data.
- Describe the app accurately as a demo; do not make unsupported medical claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and other disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.
For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead. Reports will be reviewed and handled as respectfully and confidentially
as possible.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep
the project safe and constructive.
