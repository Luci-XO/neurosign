Welcome, !

This screening has 8 short steps and takes about 10 minutes. Here is what you will do:

Part 1 - Voice (steps 1-4)

You will say a steady "aaah" four times.

  • Steps 1-3: three baseline recordings. They show what your normal voice looks like.
  • Step 4: one detection recording, compared with your baseline.

Part 2 - Movement (steps 5-8)

You will trace shapes with your mouse or trackpad.

  • Step 5: trace a spiral (baseline).
  • Step 6: trace a wave (baseline).
  • Step 7: draw any simple shape you like (baseline).
  • Step 8: repeat one of those shapes - the detection test, compared with your baseline.

Finally

All results are analysed and saved in one PDF report with a score for every step and a final screening result.

Tips: sit in a quiet room, use the same device for every movement step, and relax your hand on the desk.
